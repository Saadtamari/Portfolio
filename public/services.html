05 / Questions people ask
01Do you only work with Claude?

No. My production systems run Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, Groq and OpenRouter behind one gateway with fallback chains. Claude is where I go deepest, and the toolkit I teach.

02Can you work inside our existing stack?

Yes. My default is TypeScript, Node, PostgreSQL or Supabase and React, with Python for ML. Either way, I start by mapping what already exists before I suggest changing anything.

03What does an audit actually look like?

A snapshot query of what's really in your database, then failure classes with counts and a fix plan for each. The last one I ran found 5 failure classes across 9 apps and took signal throughput from 78 to 864.

04Can you show us code?

Systems I built for employers belong to them, so for those I walk you through architecture and decisions. My own projects I can show directly.

05Arabic or English?

Both. I write, teach and present in either.
